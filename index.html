THE IDEA

What we'll do

We'll start with concrete examples of AI-mediated data work, compare the terms we use to describe them, and test whether our distinctions help explain another person's example. We'll record useful connections and unresolved disagreements, alongside questions for future research.

  1. Compare examples
  2. Connect terms
  3. Test the distinctions

There will also be time to discuss changing research roles and connect with others working on similar problems.
