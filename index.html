What we'll do

We'll start with observations about changing data practices, roles, and tools. In small groups, we'll add examples to a shared Crazy Wall, cluster related ideas, and connect them to emerging research questions. The map is a starting point for discussion, not a definitive set of categories.

  1. Map what’s changing
  2. Connect research questions
  3. Reflect as a community

We’ll also make time for candid conversations about research identity, career paths, skills, and how we can support one another.
